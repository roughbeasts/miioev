Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 2026

This notice explains, in plain terms, what happens with your data when you visit miioev.app. It's written to comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the ePrivacy Directive.

The short version

  • This site uses Google Analytics, but only if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner. If you click "Decline" or simply close the banner without accepting, no analytics cookies are set and no data is sent to Google.
  • This site does not run ads or any other tracking scripts.
  • This site does not collect names, emails, or any personal information you submit — there is no form on this site.
  • The only data processing that occurs beyond your consent choice is standard web server access logging, done by our hosting provider, not by us.

Google Analytics

If you accept the cookie banner, this site loads Google Analytics (GA4), which sets cookies to distinguish visitors and measure site usage — pages viewed, time on site, general geographic region, and referring source. IP addresses are anonymized before storage. This data is processed by Google Ireland Limited and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Legal basis for this processing is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), given via the cookie banner. You can withdraw consent at any time using the "Cookie Settings" link in the site footer, which resets your choice and re-shows the banner on your next visit.

Google Analytics data collected through this site is used solely to understand how many people find this information useful and where they come from — never to sell products, retarget you with ads, or build an individual profile for any purpose beyond aggregate site statistics.

Hosting and server logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. (a subsidiary of Microsoft Corporation). Like virtually all web hosts, GitHub's infrastructure automatically logs standard technical data for every request — including IP address, browser type, referring page, and timestamp — for security, abuse prevention, and service operation. We (the operator of this site) do not have access to this log data and do not process it ourselves.

For details on what GitHub itself collects and how long it's retained, see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Google Fonts

This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). Depending on your browser and settings, this may result in your device making a request to a Google server, which can expose your IP address to Google under Google's own privacy policy. No font-related cookies are set by this integration.

Legal basis for processing

Minimal, incidental technical logging by our host or font provider (not by us directly) is carried out under the legitimate interest basis (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) — specifically, keeping the site operational and secure. Google Analytics, which does require consent under the ePrivacy Directive, only runs after you actively accept the cookie banner — see "Google Analytics" above.

Your rights under GDPR

You can withdraw analytics consent at any time via "Cookie Settings" in the footer. Beyond your consent choice, this site does not collect or store personal data on its own systems, so there is generally nothing further for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf. If you believe otherwise, or have any question about this notice, contact legal@miioev.app.

Changes to this notice

If this site's data practices change further, this notice will be updated accordingly.
